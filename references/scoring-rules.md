# Job Scoring Rules

Total: 100 points.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence to inspect |
|---|---:|---|
| Fixed base salary and compensation | 25 | Explicit monthly base salary, bonus/commission separation, benefits |
| Role responsibility match | 25 | Product ownership, launch, Listing/SEO, ads, profit analysis, autonomy |
| Company quality and business | 20 | Business model, products, supply chain, team and operational maturity |
| Management and growth | 15 | Mentoring, team lead scope, decision rights, promotion path |
| Work conditions and benefits | 10 | Workdays, hours, social insurance, housing fund, leave |
| Category and marketplace match | 5 | Amazon US/EU and apparel relevance |

## Salary Gate

- Fixed base >= RMB 12,000/month: passes salary gate, subject to verification.
- Fixed base < RMB 12,000/month: fails hard gate.
- Base unknown or only total compensation shown: pending HR verification, not a pass.

Do not compensate for a failed hard gate by adding points elsewhere.

## Bands

- A: 80–100 and hard gates pass; key facts substantially verified.
- B: 70–79 and hard gates pass; worth further discussion.
- C: promising but key information incomplete; verify before investing.
- Exclude: hard-gate failure or clear mismatch.

A score is a structured comparison aid, not a probability of receiving an offer.
